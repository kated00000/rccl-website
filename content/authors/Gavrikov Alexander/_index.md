The main areas of scientific activity are numerical modeling of the atmosphere, ocean, and sea state, as well as the study of turbulent processes in geophysical media at various spatial and temporal scales. Special attention is paid to the mechanisms of interaction between the ocean and the atmosphere, the processes of turbulent exchange at the interface between media, and the formation and evolution of coherent vortex structures.

 He is engaged in teaching at the Department of Oceanology of the Faculty of Geography at Lomonosov Moscow State University and teaches a course of lectures on “Dynamic Meteorology”.
